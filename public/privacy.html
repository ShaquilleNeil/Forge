Forge privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

What Forge is

Forge is a personal training log by S.Neil. It keeps your program, your workouts and your numbers in one place, and lets a coach you link review them.

What we collect

Your account: your name and email address, from Google sign-in or the email you sign up with. Your profile: display name, units (kg or lbs), bar weight, plate set, rest times and main lifts. Your training data: programs, workouts, sets, reps, RPE, bar speed, bodyweight entries and personal records. Videos you attach to sets (at most 5 minutes each). Coach links and pairing codes, when you connect with a coach. Spreadsheets: when you import a training spreadsheet, or turn on sheet write-back, Forge reads — and, for write-back, writes to — the Google Sheet you pick. Only that sheet, and only when you ask.

How we use it

Only to run the app: your log, your programs, your coach link, your write-back to your own sheet. Forge has no advertising, no analytics, and never sells your data.

Where it is stored

Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage), in the developer’s Firebase project. Your data stays in your own account inside that project.

Who it is shared with

Nobody — except a coach you link. While linked, that coach can see your workouts and programs. You can unlink them at any time from My coach or Clients, and nothing else leaves your account.

Your choices

Export your workouts as a CSV from Settings. Delete everything — your account, your programs, your workouts, your videos — from Settings → Delete account. Unlink a coach from My coach or Clients.

Contact and changes

Questions or corrections: contact@example.com. If this policy changes, the new version appears here.
